Map key & limits

  • No current unprotected attackers

  • Your selected tile

Mob sizes, wave rosters, arena and spawn points are based on documented Inferno data; Jad spawn tiles are starting estimates. Safe tiles are estimated from current positions, attack range, the selected prayer, and tile LOS. This is a planning aid—not a tick simulator. It does not model pathing, attack cycles, dynamic blob timing, Jad attack cues/healers, pillar damage, or Zuk wave 69. Check positions against the game.

No connection to the OSRS client is used; move each monster on the map to match your current wave. Setup is saved in this browser.
